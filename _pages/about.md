I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), and a member of the HKUST NLP Group. I am supervised by Prof. Junxian He, who also advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), from which I graduated in June 2024.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include:
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
